# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, personal or political attacks,
trolling, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in
issues, discussions, PRs, or any project space.

Give feedback about code, not people. Assume good intent; disagree with
evidence. Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violates
this, and issues/PRs may be closed when discussion stops being useful.

That's the whole policy. It applies to everyone, maintainers included.
